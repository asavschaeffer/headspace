Freewrite

I saw a clip on Twitter this morning of a monkey taking a snake corpse and draping it about his neck "like a cuban link" right in the face of another snake. As the monkey turned its back, and sat there playing with his new accessory, the snake just bobbed back and forth and tasted the air. It got me thinking about the snake and the monkey and their mental situations.

First off, it suggested to me immediately that this snake was confused or bewildered or in my mental words "It reached the last line of instructions in its script".

That led me to think about my comparisons between possessed, animated machines and living creatures. How could I make a statement by showing a person the similarities between this little snake who wasn't prepared for that situation, or wasn't cognizing the offensive gravity of it, and a little worm bot excavator drill or conveyor belt-chan cutely moving rocks from nowhere to nowhere. What I mean to say is that we find it cute to anthropomorphize, and I want to take advantage of this and put non-human characters in positions that show the emotion: "this wasn't in the script" and "I don't have instructions to control my behavior in this scenario". Or, quite possibly, as in the case of the snake and the monkey "I am not aware of the dimension in which the significance of this situation is housed".

If the word dimension there is confusingly chosen, allow me to provide a definition and example. A dimension is a lateral mode of awareness wherein all observations are made. Organized vertically, the bottom dimension is the essential sense of pain, somewhere above that are the 5 senses of touch, taste, sight, smell, and hearing. Above the senses are the judgements, ranging from biased, false, and critical to exacting, true, and observational. In the case of the monkey and the snake, I would guess at the monkey's dimension of awareness to be focused on the sensations of sight, touch, and smell, while he is handling the snake, and perhaps a higher dimension of self-satisfaction, aesthetic sensibility, or general relaxation and playfulness. It's possible still, that the monkey is operating at the higher still level of disrespecting the corpse, tormenting the other snake, and brazenly turning his back in an act of goading evil.

The snake is the intriguing mystery. The range of dimensions it could be aware of are not clearly indicated by its behavior. On the one hand, the look that it must be burning into the back of the monkey's head, plus the swaying motion, and raised up head suggest at the very least a tendency towards violence. Maybe there is an awareness of danger to the snake because of the way the monkey handles another snake? Maybe there is an awareness of the disrespect of the turning of the back? It could be the case that the snake is aggrieved at the treatment of his dead kin. But, I think just as possible, from the limited evidence, the snake is totally unaware of the gravity of the situation and is just in a basic observational state.

So to map out the situation and the possibilities:

  1. The snake is totally unaware of the monkey's disrespectful behavior
    1. The snake is bakawaii
  2. The snake is somewhat aware of the monkey's disrespectful behavior
    1. Low: snake fixated on an animal with their back turned, which is an essential predator behavior
    2. Medium: snake fixated on the corpse of the snake as a threat to itself
  3. The snake is fully aware
    1. The snake understands that the monkey is blatantly using a fresh corpse as a toy, and is ruminating on attacking it out of anger, trying to think about what to do

I'm struggling now to articulate the deeper significance of the situation, because I know it isn't that different animals have different levels of awareness of the experiential dimensions of life. My original thought was about programming: that this snake is behaving like a little robot, scripted for a limited set of situations and caught without clear instructions.

That led me to the Sol Eremus "world pillar" (name in progress), where at the top there is savage garden, "upper yard" (name in progress), where the primary obstacles are mighty and noble beasts and twisting roots and vines. At the bottom there are twisting caves and dark pits, inhabited by cursed creatures, lure plants, and possessed and berserk machinery. But this situation gave me the thought: what if, when one looks very closely, one finds that the machinery is scripted just like the noble creatures are? As above so below, as it were. Animals are little programs, and that is true in some way, but their programming is complex and nuanced and created over million years of trial and error.

So the application for me, in the end, is that I need to find ways to express this bakawaii, this feeling of seeing a simple creature, at a loss for words, or a loss for actions, when it is confronted with a situation it was not prepared for, or when it is operating at some dimension and the real significance of the situation is not at that dimension.
