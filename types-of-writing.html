Nonfiction

Musashi Miyamoto wrote in the The Book of 5 Rings "the warrior always chooses death", and that "fear of self-preservation prevents proper service of one's lord." Now what is a lord to the reader surely is different from what is meant by Miyamoto, but the usefulness of the message is only diluted, not nullified.

Persuasive

Living like a warrior in 2024 by serving the lord with selfless abandon is the best way to achieve. Our desire to provide for ourselves, increase our prestige, and better our lot is actually holding us back from doing our best work. The truth exists regardless of how well or poorly we are doing or feeling. Approaching that truth is the best way to improve the situation of the world.

Dialectic

What are some situations where this phenomenon can be easily exemplified?

Overcoming fatigue and pain to help others and increase the overall utility returns for all players in any given game state

I can see how choosing to experience pain would be "choosing death" in a metaphorical sense. But who would be the lord in this case?

The lord is all who benefit from the results of the game. In other words, the fruits of the labor. In the utilitarian game theory, if the positive results for some players is greater than the negative results for yourself, and even potentially equal to or lesser than, the way of the warrior is to engage in the game regardless and wholeheartedly.

The best lord to choose is one's partner, children, parents, or company.

Wouldn't that lead to an extremely unhappy life? To always choose pain and "death"?

No. What actually happens is a baseline reset for what is pain and death, establishing it as lower than the standard existence. When that happens, moments of experience for the standard existence are elevated, relative to the experience of pain and death, and because moments of experience for the standard experience are common, a greater amount of time spent in states of elevated experience is appreciated.

It sounds like the life is altogether worse but actually appreciated more.

Exactly, and not only that but it uplifts the lives of all who engage in games with it as well. Outside of experientially, the approach increases success in the actual ventures resulting from the toil of choosing pain and death. The results of that toil would be of the highest quality, because the concern for well-being being put aside for the sake of the lord enables greater commitment to the completion of the task. People will definitely face higher success to the things that they are committed to, and the degree of commitment will increase the quality of the effort and potential of success.

Worldbuilding

Out in the desert, or high in the mountains, an old clan of warrior mercenary guardians continues an epic cycle of training, teaching, and questing. They are shields-for-hire, skilled in the art of war. A contract is created only to buy the services of a shield for life, and therefore services are rendered complete upon the death of the mercenary. Upon death these shield bearers are frozen in crystal granite, sealing their skills away, to be awarded to the next clanmember to reach the place of death. These crystal tombs create powerful fields in service of the intention of the dead.

Narrative

One such warrior mercenary guardian was hired to protect a family, a mother, father, and child. They were on a journey to reach the location of the child's dreams, for he was clearly a type of prophet, and had a tendency to go off without warning. The task of the warrior was actually to follow the child's mother, who was absolutely fixated on the protection of her child and would frantically endanger herself unnecessarily due to the curious nature of the child.

The group of four traveled across the Coast of Midin, a popular trading and cultural hub of the Eastern Seas. One town was under duress by bandits in the hills, and then was pounced on by greedy seafaring traders. They couldn't access their favorite commodities from the typical land routes because of the bandits, and were forced to pay unfair prices to the seafaring traders.

Wherever the child went, mother followed, and the guardian carefully dispatched any threats to the child. The child takes to the mountains, following a butterfly. The bandits are greatly excited, and ambushed the group, only to be embroiled in a massive and costly battle. The truth is that the child is young and naive, but nimble and intelligent. A child doesn't understand the consequences of actions, but understands the order of events. Therefore, it is foolish to assume one will just be a victim in the face of some difficult challenges. The mother and mercenary generate massive power through selfless defense, and the raw destructive power of a father fighting for the life of his wife and child is unmatched.

The coincidental path chosen by the child causes the bandits to release their strategic hold of key mountain passages blocking the military assistance sent by the larger inland city states. The military men, in their gregarious manner, spread out through the Coastal towns. The seafaring traders, having lost their supremacy, reveal an unscrupulous alliance by rescuing the last of the fleeing bandits at the windy beach near Isla Nostra.
