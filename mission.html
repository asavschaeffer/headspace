Mission

Our goal is to supply software and hardware for the use of interactive systems to teach, stimulate, and uplift. Gadgets track physical activity and vitals, our software, powered by concurrence tokenization, progresses the virtual characters relative to their real-life action. This *RPGification* is shown by the billion dollar research to improve motivation. For a game developer, gathering data about a user's physicality gives incredible opportunity to create engaging experience.

Essentially I have a dream to make a really great game: I want to reinstate a connection to the divine with a game about a great pilgrimage, inspire people to get up and do real physical activity by gamifying exercise/work, and create a rhythmic system where in game actions combined with npcs make a sloppy little song.

To make that dream a reality I am learning a lot of different game development skills. Essentially I am overspread as a "full stack game developer". I am my own writer, designer, programmer, tester, project manager, artist, sound, marketer.

This is fine for me. I love learning about different things, applying myself to different problems, learning constantly.

Employers, naturally, are not looking for this. Sure, there is value in an employee who can learn different skills, but companies hire to solve a problem. They have a role that needs to be filled, a specific job that needs to be done. If they need many different jobs filled, they will hire many different people, each an expert in their discipline.

So that puts me in a position, if I want to join the industry to choose something to specialize in. My long term plan currently is to produce indie games as I develop my game development and marketing skillset. I will attempt to use those to get a game designer or writer position. If I cannot, I will double down and implement AI, Web3, and Multiplayer technology and position myself as an AI researcher. If that fails I will double down as an AI researcher and pivot industries. If that fails I will double down as a game developer and try to release a super profitable game. If that fails I will try again. If that fails I will try again. If that fails I will cook full time. If that fails I will be a yoga teacher. If that fails I will become a ascetic hermit.
