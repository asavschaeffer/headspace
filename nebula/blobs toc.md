## Introduction

blobs essentially is a #shortform idea reposit box. It can reorganize your previous messages in a veriety of useful ways. Combining, sharing and incorporating content in new ways provides value to users. 

## Usage

1) a user enters a text and/or file into the box. They may attach any #label they so choose, but this is not absolutely necessary. Markdown is supported and encouraged.
2) a user can open the box and browse the text and/or file, which now has been combined into a blob. 
3) Files are largely unlimited by type, with some exceptions such as suspicious downloads, excessively large files, or encrypted untraceable files.
4) when the box is open the blobs can be combined and new content is generated. The scope of the source blobs is variable according to the user.
5) Overtime, when greater clusters of blobs develop, the AI integration can prove massively useful as an index, assistant, co-author, debate opponent, teacher, student, researcher, etc.
6) View other users and share your thoughts with them, and incorporate their thoughts into your blobs. A group of blobs is called a Nebula. 
7) Basically its twitter with less limited file uploads, slightly longer post lengths, more accessible/personal hashtags, and #AI integrated retweets.

## Blob

a blob is constructed with a h1 for a title, and then the corresponding tweet. this can be organzied into headings and contain a great deal of information, or something very brief, but essentially it must be about only one topic, with any connections to other topics directly connected. If there are enough connections to a specific topic, a connection blob will quickly be made automatically to continue those thoughts.
