## You own your character

You can purchase a run at the game, which is your character, for 25 cents. You can purchase a feather of protection, for $2.00 equivalent. You can queue up with your friends by typing their names into a grouping hat, after you create your character? But before you enter the game. Once you die, without a feather of protection, your character is gone forever. Feathers are purchasable at any time. The pricing scheme increases exponentially per feather purchased in bulk. However, they are always $2.00 to purchase one more.

All items, skills, possessions, fashion, in game objects, etc. are minted and linked with blockchain technology. All items for purchase in the in-game shop are also digital items that can be worn by the character and the player IRL. Likewise, the feather of protection will be sent to the player IRL for every one purchased. Some ideas are: piece of incense, stick of eremurus plant (wild CA wheat), bird feather,

Moreover, the combination of minted items that cluster together in one run under a person’s name is their avatar and belongings, which can be bundled together and sold on the free market. The game will be supported by taxing this market, as well as merch like hoodies, t-shirts, and hats for brand promotion.
