## After

[[The Deity Becomes Illuminated and self-reflective]]
"maybe at first there was **nothing**, which eventually must have been introduced to light or dark. With that conception, **something** became illuminated and whatever deity was there to govern it all experienced **everything** as self-reflective".

[[Casting out all that it saw and disliked]]

Much of what was illuminated and self-reflected was found to be disagreeable, and promptly cast away as profane. In this situation, from such parentage, a deity wholly of rejection was born: Hajrudin.

[[A deity of rejection manifests itself]]

This deities' power was immediately manifested as a powerful physical repulsion from the original illuminated deity. Hurtling through space, a desire to stabilize and stop found expression in great spiney rocks, gigantic in size and mass, appearing to serve as a force of friction with which to brutally collide. Through some incredible measure of rock some loveless creature now agonizes at the bottom of.

[[What was left was wholly good]]

Back at what is now the center gently, over aeons, taking his own time, coalesces into a child of, or a reformed revision of, the self-illuminated deity. This one thinks that his other half probably needs his help, and sets out to find him. Checking under each and every overturned stone, employing the aid of every noble and elegant creature of earth, effortlessly fashioning steps as he went, Havaldar raised up a thorough road. This took him an eternity.

[[Reunification]]
Havaldar stands at the edge of a great pit, seeing the obscured form of Hajrudin through the darkness.
