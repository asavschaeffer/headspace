# topographical cycle of mountains and rivers


## taking Surfliner up to L.A

I noticed that every 10 minutes or so there is a marshy area, with a bridge over a body of water heading out into the ocean. After the marshy area, and train climbs and we enter a dry mesa, the shrubs and half buried rocks holding the cliffs up. 

### marsh

the #marshland is made up of an estuary, could be brackish or clear. It's largely standing water but it will swell and contract with the tides.

### cliffs

the #cliff is red-brown dirt, elegantly eroded in a pattern of #valley and #mountain - a fractal of sorts - which seems to defy gravity as it towers over the train tracks. The shrubs and grasses give the impression that the area is very hot.
