SMITE Charon Redesign

Date: 2025-03-25 | Status: Concept | Project: N/A | 60% Complete

Initial Thoughts

This character is a very depressing design. I don’t want to hate on SMITE—it really is one of my longest-time and closest-to-my-heart games—but let’s talk about the potential for a skeleton boatman, the myth of Charon, and how Hi-Rez could improve the character.

The Skeletal Boatman

Charon, as currently designed, is a water mage. There’s nothing in his character that suggests he has any role as a ferryman, a skeleton, or even anything related to death. His kit consists of:

  • A line skillshot
  • A ranged AoE skillshot
  • A charge
  • A big AoE charge-up ultimate

Nothing in that kit, besides maybe the big AoE ultimate, really fits a guardian personally. I guess the charge could fit a supporting role, but come on—you didn’t incorporate any “ferrying” with the charge? Even He Bo gives speed to his allies with his water scroll. You’re telling me Charon, the ferrying boatman, doesn’t help his allies move forward—let alone let them ride along—as he frantically paddles straight at his enemies like a brainless, brainrot character? It’s a completely missed opportunity.

As a ferryman, there’s nothing in his kit to suggest this role at all.

Proposed Redesign

Here’s how I propose to redesign the character to better reflect his mythos:

  • Incorporate the skeleton
  • Incorporate the ferrying
  • Channel the myth of Charon

The Myth of Charon

Charon is the ferryman into the underworld over the River Styx. Hermes guides the dead to the river’s shores, and they present the coin—the obol—that was placed in their mouths before their bodies were consumed by the funeral pyre. Charon takes the souls across the river so they might find their eternal resting place in the land of Hades, after their judgment.

As the skeletal guide of the dead, three water spells and a rowing charge just don’t convey the depth of this character. It’s such a badass title, and it’s incredibly easy to brainstorm ideas for. Yet Hi-Rez dropped the ball.

Redesign Details

Passive: Ferryman of the Dead

Charon’s current passive is a banking system where he gets coins. It’s boring and lazy. Hi-Rez thinks it’s justified because it’s “thematic”—related to the death of minions and players, like he’s doing his ferryman role on the battlefield. But really, it’s just a pathetic loot simulator, like Thresh in League of Legends. Let’s do this thoroughly:

Charon is the ferryman of the dead—he doesn’t kill people, he takes them “across the river.” When Charon gets an assist, he can:

  1. Take their obol
  2. Take them across the river

Here are some passive ideas, ranked by fun factor:

  • Most Unfun: Charon can carry resurrecting allies back to the battlefield, respawning them.
  • Medium: Charon escorts players back to the fountain after death—helping friendly players, hindering enemies—interfering with resurrection times.
  • Fun: Charon takes an obol from enemies he kills or assists with, converted to 300g at the shop and tradable with allies.
  • Best: Charon picks up allies or enemies whose death he was involved in and ferries them on his boat, forcing their death camera to the back of Charon’s boat perspective.

Ability 1: Power Turn (Melee/Ranged Option)

Nowadays, the “1” is a bread-and-butter ranged option for every character in SMITE. It didn’t use to be so uninspired. For a MOBA, it’s more important to have a good piloting feel than uniformity across champions. Charon should have little to no range—he’s a boat ferryman. His paddle and boat are his weapons, so let’s make it more than slapping people with a paddle or splashing them with water.

To paddle a canoe involves steering and accelerating with the same paddle. Holding it at the back, the paddle can glide the boat to either side. Stroking it through the water turns it sharply based on direction and side. Here’s a realistic implementation:

  • Momentum: The boat has momentum—gaining speed straight ahead but drifting toward the paddling side. Stopping isn’t instant.
  • Controls: W/^ and S/v ready the paddle and stroke. A/< and D/> choose the side. E.g., W -> A strokes from top-left to bottom-left, moving the boat forward and right.
  • Math:
    • Forward speed increases with boat speed up to a cap: 0 mph -> 5 mph, 5 -> 11, 11 -> 20, 20 -> 22 (flatlines).
    • Sideways speed decreases with boat speed: 0 mph -> 3 mph (curve y=√x to 5,3), 5 mph -> 2 mph, 10 -> 1, 20 -> 0.7, 22 -> 0.25.
    • Backwards strokes (e.g., S -> D at 22 mph) reduce forward speed by 15 and turn hard to the paddling side.
  • Paddling Rhythm: One stroke unless held, creating a rhythm.

“Power Turn” is a deep swipe (left or right, back to front) in three parts:

  1. Swipe pushes through water, displacing the boat opposite the chosen side.
  2. Nose rises and crashes down (WASD-directed), with a big knockup, damage, tremble, and River Styx debuff.
  3. Tail rocks up and down, causing displacement and splashing River Styx water.

Sweet spot at the paddle’s end deals damage and drags targets with the paddle’s direction. Closer to Charon reduces damage but applies minor displacement. Early sweet spot pushes targets into Styx water buff/debuff; late sweet spot smashes targets into the hull (knockup + tremble).

Ability 2: Ferry (Buff Spell)

“Ferry” increases Charon’s momentum cap based on players riding him, emphasizing his ferryman role.

River Styx Water Effect

River Styx water could:

  • Extend ability cooldowns
  • Lock the ability on cooldown until the debuff expires
  • Block the next available ability

Final Thoughts

Thematically, Charon is a tiny player in the mythos—a servant in Hades’ realm. It’s beyond obvious that Hi-Rez doesn’t have a mythologist of any pedigree working for them—an absolutely hilarious fact evident since they never did. Someone with deep mythological knowledge could’ve had a massive positive impact on SMITE since its genesis over 10 years ago. Imagine where it’d be if it truly championed the world’s gods well. Oh well, I was only 12 then.
