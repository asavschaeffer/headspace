Stacked Disks World

A world like many stacked disks is created by a family schism of the primordial gods. Resulting from the reconciliatory efforts of one brother towards the other, the purehearted denizens of the world are exposed to a cessblood of evil and infected, leaving the world in a state like an unmixed potion, sediment falling down through the cracks into the base of the world, and those light enough to float live more peacefully at the top.
Many years passed and the whole world was established. The gentle forest creatures who were created to follow Havaldar were touched by the boiling rageblood of Hajrudin and developed monstrous features that can be creatively explored as a statement about technology and nature, the separation/continuity between humanity and nature, and the cruel parts of nature such as stingers, claws, barbs, thorns, fangs, poisons, gore, blood, bones, muscles and flesh. The bottom most floors are populated by more humanlike foes but at the same time more machinery, imagine if we put the Warhammer megacities combined with the time and level design of DS1 Undead Burg.

Across the land there was a custom to travel the swirling sea, the mountain at its center, climb and reach the highest world, beyond the clouds, where one might convene with the immortal solar deity. Different cultures and cults held different beliefs, but the prevailing sentiment at the time of the year 2,000 was that the deity was immortal and powerful and protected the people of the world from the dark shades and the disease that made machinery animated and berserk.

The tradition was to embark on a pilgrimage, which was always supported by the primary solar cult, to commune with the deity at the top of the world. But while many pilgrims left their homes to perform this ritual, none returned. The belief of the primary solar cult was that they were enjoying the glorious light of the deity and couldn’t bear to leave. Some people had doubts, and the journey was certainly recognized as treacherous, but the influence and dogma of the primary solar cult was powerful.

This was the state of the world in the year 2,000. Different countries on the continents and islands of the swirling sea do trade and war. There was a desert continent, a mountainous one, tropical island nations, fertile plains and forests. The ocean that connected these different places always swirled in one direction, and every day or so it would reverse. The citizens of the world lived, partly in reverence and hope towards the solar deity that trickled its illumination upon the world, partly in fear of the dark shades and twisted machines that emerged from the bowels of the earth during the night. In the sea, enormous monsters were kept at bay by two mysterious seafaring cults, rivals, who are employed by merchants and pirates alike to ensure safe passage in the deep ocean.

Their weaponry were single and double shot rifles, cannons, swords and shields. They smithed with steel and iron. Seafaring was more important to the coastal communities, but supported and funded by the aristocrats in each country and supplied by the forestry industry mainly of one or two continents.

The world is organized such that at the center of the world, the weather and environment is harsher, the monsters are more frequent, crawling out of the great pit wherein all the ocean’s water is being drained into. The further one gets from the center, the greater the impression of untouched nature, and less the of the mark of industrialization and machinery.

Ever since the desert mining mountain of Dur Dinandin had their original uprising, where their mining machinery came to life and attacked the miners, machines across the land became potential hosts to a corruption, causing fear and death among the people who worked with them. That desert city, a jewel in the country, fell and the country itself collapsed. Merchants scarcely visit, for fear of the rampant mining machinery, but the pilgrims to the solar deity always visit; the desert is very close to the center of the world and is home to the greatest and biggest church/site of pilgrimage/staging ground before the first ascent. No matter one’s background, the primary solar cult will support and supply for a pilgrim.

There is rumor that also present on that island is the house of guardian mercenaries that enact a heavy price for an elite bodyguard. These "runeshield" vanguard warriors are hired for life, and wield a powerful protective magic and armor. They are binded by a powerful magic, which peaks at the time of death, to protect and facilitate the goals of the client. When a runeshield dies, their body is covered in clear crystal, a beautiful tomb, only to be dissipated by contact with another runeshield warrior, wherein the memories of the dead one flood into the living, along with their skills, magic, and experience. These crystal tombs grant blessings to the area around them. Somewhere in that desert, the headquarters of these warriors can be found.
