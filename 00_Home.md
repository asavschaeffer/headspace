# Home

This document serves as the central hub for the Globule project wiki.

## Directory Structure

The wiki is organized into the following directories, reflecting the project's creative and developmental lifecycle:

*   **Vision/**: The highest-level goals and direction.
    *   `vision-strategy.md`
    *   `Project-Roadmap.md`

*   **Design/**: The architectural blueprints and high-level plans.
    *   `01_High_Level_Design_Document.md`
    *   `architectural philosophy hld narrative.md`
    *   `Technical-Architecture.md`

*   **Engineering/**: The concrete, low-level implementation details.
    *   `LDD-shopping-list.md`
    *   `LLD-modules/`

*   **Research/**: The exploratory work and information gathering.
    *   `LLD_research/`

*   **Archive/**: Inactive or historical documents.
    *   `old/`
    *   `roleplays/`

The `Cathedral-of-Recursive-Understanding.md` remains at the root, serving as the project's constitution.
